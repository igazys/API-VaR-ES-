The calculations use the most recent days of history. 500 days is about two years.

What you will get

Value-at-Risk (VaR) answers: “On all but the worst 1 in 100 days, how much could I lose at most?” Expected Shortfall (ES) answers the follow-up: “And when one of those worst days does happen, how much do I lose on average?”

No single method gives the true answer, so the page shows five. Where they disagree tells you something about the portfolio, such as fat tails or rising volatility.
